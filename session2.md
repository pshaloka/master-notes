# Mythic Bastionland

### Session 2 Outline 

1. Conversation with Ally 
    - What will you do with me before selling me off like a prized brood mare? 
    - Ally wants to shame/convince the knights to let her masquerade as a peasant woman and stay with them during their travels 
    - point of the scene: develop Ally and endear her to the knights, force the knights to begin to choose between duty and Ally 
2. Travel through the Willowgrass 
    - light rain, banks of fog rolling through the landscape like leviathans in the deep 
    - small circle of stone menhirs, the remains of some large animal in the center, untouched by scavengers 
    - luck roll to hear the whispers of the dead (learn more about the knights)
3. Duel by the ponds 
    - restless spirit of a sergeant who lead his men into a trap, begs the knights to help their shades depart in peace
    - someone needs to duel and vanquish the Moat Knight, who lost his life to Jurgen's trap
    - the Moat Knight stalks the shallows of the ponds, preventing the ghosts from moving on and passing into the waters 
4. Arriving at Verdensward
    - you see a stone marker for Greenfields, the wooden sign that seems to have covered it lies splintered beneath 
    - pass through rows of wildflowers and apiaries: a curious group of children begins to follow the knights and offer top braid flowers into the manes of the knights mounts 
    - 8 foot high stone wall surrounds the town, set amidst gentle hills
    - old town of stone, new construction of brick and clay, maybe about 5,000 people live here. 
    - votive candles flicker in laterns hung from most doors
    - wealthier, larger homes decorated with wax masks set about trees carved into the stone or brick facades of the homes 
    - Miller's local open-air gathering hall, tables are large, broken millstoes in various states of disrepair 
    - Gilded Lily is a nicer inn 
5. Court of the Turtle Knight 
    - filled with children
    - preparing for the upcoming festival 
    - wants to show everyone a good time, worried about troubling reports of the Dead from the south: really wants the knights to go see the Seer and take care of it
    - will wine and dine them, take them on a hunt, anything to encourage them to take care of the Dead as soon as possible
6. Around Town 
    - Hans, the armorer: not truly a blacksmith, but a guildmaster and craftsmen slowly introducing Seapeople tech 
    - shield made from Blushbough, painted on the inside with an image of Saint Sara the Shieldmaiden (if you are fatigued, you look at the image of Saint Sara to steady your resolve, gain two free gambits for the rest of combat) -> wants the players to give him the spoils of a hunt from the Turtle Knight
    - Brown Ben, who runs the apiarys, lots of local knowledge, tentatively supports the local Queensmen due to a mislike of the modern ways of the Seapeople, has a great club, willing to trade for a different weapon, or a promise to seek out the Talon Knight and help him, sells sticky jars of honey that will slow movement
7. On the Hunt 
    - test rolls to see if they get anything, and how good that something could be
    - ambush? by a band of the Queensmen, who want to kill the Turtle Knight (presents a moral quandary/opportunity for the players!)
